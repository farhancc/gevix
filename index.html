What we build

Five kinds of work, one team. Everything starts with design and most of it carries straight on into engineering, so the people who drew the screens are the ones who ship them.

Web apps

Customer portals, marketplaces, internal tools. Built in React and Next.js with the accessibility and performance work done during the build, not filed for later.

Mobile apps

iOS and Android from one codebase with React Native, or native Swift and Kotlin when the app needs the hardware. We handle App Store and Play Store submission.

SaaS platforms

Multi-tenant products with billing, roles, onboarding and an admin your support team can actually use. We design the pricing page and the upgrade flow too.

Websites and web design

Marketing sites, brand sites and launch pages that load fast and read well on a phone. Designed in Figma, built in Framer or Next.js, with a CMS your team can edit without us.

SEO

Technical SEO on everything we ship: clean markup, fast Core Web Vitals, structured data and a sensible site map. Plus keyword research and a content plan when you want to grow search traffic on purpose.

Already have engineers? We design, test with users, and hand off a Figma library with a written spec your team can build from.

The same people draw it and build it. Nothing gets lost in between.Most products get worse at handoff: the designer moves on, the engineer guesses. At Gevix the designer sits in the pull request and the engineer sits in the Figma file, from kickoff to launch.

Questions we get asked

If yours isn't here, the first call is free and we'll answer it there.

Do you work with early-stage companies?

Yes. About half our projects are first versions for funded startups. We'll tell you honestly if a two-week design sprint is a better first step than a full build.

Can you take over an existing product?

Yes. We start with a two-week audit of the codebase and the design, then propose what to keep, what to fix and what to rebuild, with a cost for each.

Who owns the code and the design files?

You do, from day one. Code lives in your GitHub organisation and the Figma files sit in your workspace. If we part ways, nothing needs to be transferred.

How do you price a project?

Fixed-scope projects get a fixed price after discovery. Monthly teams are a flat rate per person per month. We don't bill by the hour.

Is SEO included, or extra?

The technical side is included in every website and web app: semantic markup, fast load times, structured data, meta tags and a site map. Keyword research, content planning and ongoing optimisation are a separate monthly engagement.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of fixes are included. After that, most clients keep a smaller monthly team; the rest take a full handover with documentation and a recorded walkthrough.
