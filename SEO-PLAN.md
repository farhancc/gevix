# Gevix SEO plan

Baseline audit ran 19 Sep 2026 (day the domain went live): health score ~42/100.
Everything fixable in code shipped the same day. What's left needs real content, accounts, and time.

## Done (19 Sep 2026)

| Finding | Fix |

## Needs you — in priority order

1. **Replace placeholder proof.** Client names (Ledgerly, Northloop, Cartwright, Halden Studio, Pillar Health, Nordwall Films, Bright & Co, Marlow Interiors), all five testimonials, every outcome number, the "12 products since 2019 / 91% return" stats band, and the four author names. Pillar Health in particular makes healthcare/NHS claims. Until this is real, do not push for indexing or links — fabricated specifics that get cached by AI answer engines are hard to retract.
2. **Confirm the facts that stayed:** founded 2019, eleven people, based in India, the phone number, European/US hours.
3. **Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools.** Verify `gevix.in`, submit `sitemap.xml`. Then add a Google API key to `~/.config/claude-seo/google-api.json` so future audits get field Core Web Vitals.
4. **Expand the two articles that can rank** to 1,200–1,600 words each: *The handoff problem* and *The SEO work that's just good engineering* (SERP-overlap analysis found these are the only two with a real cluster around them). Leave the rest short; they read well as notes.
5. **Author bios.** A one-paragraph bio, a photo, and a LinkedIn URL per author, then `Person` schema with `sameAs`. Without this the blog has no E-E-A-T anchor.
6. **Entity presence.** LinkedIn company page, Google Business Profile (even without a public address), Clutch/GoodFirms listings. Put the URLs in the Organization schema's `sameAs`.
7. **A real newsletter provider** (Buttondown or similar) if you want the subscribe band back.

## Content architecture (from the cluster analysis)

Six blog categories don't map to six search clusters. Collapse to three:

- **How we build** (Process + Design) → hub: a 3,000-word *Design-to-development handoff* guide at `/blog/design-to-development-handoff`. Spokes: existing handoff post, design-sprint post, plus *design system handoff documentation* and *design QA checklist before release*.
- **Sites & search** (Websites + SEO) → hub: *SEO checklist for a new website launch* at `/blog/seo-checklist-new-website-launch`. Spokes: existing SEO post (retarget to "SEO for developers" — the most winnable query found), fast-marketing-sites post, plus *Core Web Vitals checklist* and *website redesign without losing SEO*.
- **Product notes** (Engineering + SaaS) → no hub; link each post from the matching service on the home page instead.

Don't build category archive pages — with 1–2 posts each they're thin. Don't chase "software development agency India" with articles; that SERP is directories and listicles, so it's won via `/work` and listings.

Cadence: one expanded post or spoke every two weeks. Hubs first.

## Backlinks (starting from zero)

1. Directory listings: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, plus Indian directories once the address is public.
2. A "Built by Gevix" credit link in the footer of every client site, with the client's permission.
3. Author profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub, Dribbble) linking to gevix.in.
4. Guest pieces on DEV.to and Medium republishing the SEO/engineering posts with canonical back to gevix.in.
5. No paid links, no link exchanges, no PBNs.
